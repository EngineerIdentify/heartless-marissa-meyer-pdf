## 📖 Overview

**Heartless** is a 2016 young adult fantasy novel by **Marissa Meyer**, author of *The Lunar Chronicles*. It serves as a **prequel origin story** for the Queen of Hearts from Lewis Carroll's *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*.

The novel reimagines the infamous villain as a **sympathetic young woman** named Cath — a talented baker with dreams of opening her own shop and falling in love. Through a series of tragic events, she transforms into the cold-hearted monarch readers know from Carroll's classic.

## 📜 Synopsis

Cath is the daughter of the **Marquess of Mock Turtle** and dreams of a life far from the royal court. She wants to open a bakery with her best friend and maid, **Jest** — the new court joker — has captured her heart.

But the **King of Hearts** has other plans. He intends to propose to Cath at the upcoming **Black and White Ball**, and her parents are determined to see her become queen. As Cath navigates court politics, family pressure, and forbidden love, she discovers how cruel Wonderland can truly be.

When tragedy strikes, Cath's transformation into the feared **Queen of Hearts** begins — proving that not all villains are born; some are made.

## 💬 FAQ

**Is Heartless part of a series?**
No — it's a standalone novel.

**Do I need to read Alice in Wonderland first?**
No, but it enriches the experience.

**Is it suitable for young readers?**
Yes — recommended for ages 12+.

**Does it have a happy ending?**
No — it's a tragic origin story.

**Are there sequels?**
No, but the author has other books.

**Is there an audiobook?**
Yes — narrated by Rebecca Soler.

**What genre is it?**
YA fantasy retelling.

**Is it based on a true story?**
No — inspired by Lewis Carroll's fictional world.

**Are there translations?**
Yes — over 30 languages.

**Will there be a movie?**
No announcement as of 2025.
